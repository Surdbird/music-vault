editorial notes ↑

* Take the Coltrane — linked to the canonical 1962 Ellington & Coltrane album recording (Impulse!), not the later reissues.

* Goodbye Pork Pie Hat (Mingus) — linked to Mingus Ah Um original 1959 Columbia. Joni Mitchell's vocal version linked separately.

* In Walked Bud — linked to Monk's 1951 Blue Note recording (Genius of Modern Music Vol. 1), the definitive original.

* Django (John Lewis/MJQ) — linked to the 1954 Prestige recording. Multiple later versions exist; this is the one written on Reinhardt's death.

* I Remember Clifford — linked to Golson's own 1958 recording on Benny Golson and the Philadelphians. Lee Morgan's version is equally famous.

* He Loved Him Madly (Miles for Ellington) — 32 minutes long. Linked to the Get Up With It album track. Not easy listening; very much worth it.

* Sir Duke (Stevie Wonder) — included as a rare pop/soul crossover tribute because it is one of the most-heard jazz homages in history.

* Anthropology (Parker/Gillespie) — co-written; linked to Bird's 1945 Savoy recording. A tribute in spirit and in practice.

* Several tracks marked not on Spotify are unavailable or only appear on out-of-print compilations not currently licensed to the platform.
