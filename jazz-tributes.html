A Map of Homage — But Beautiful

Jazz as a network of tributes — songs written for, about, and in response to other musicians

These connections are not linear. They are melodic and harmonic.

Some edges here are explicit dedications — songs named for the person they honour. Others are recordings made together across generations, or absorbed influences so deep they surface as composition. In jazz, the distinction between tribute, collaboration, and inheritance is always porous. Lester Young can sound indebted to Stan Getz, who owes everything to Lester Young. The arrow of influence runs both ways, and sometimes forward in time.

Read each edge as you would a chord — not a statement of fact, but a relationship with tension and resonance.
